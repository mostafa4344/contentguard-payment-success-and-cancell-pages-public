Delete your ContentGuard account

This page explains how to ask Halal TechLife to delete your ContentGuard - Content Blocker account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the same Gmail address you use to sign in to ContentGuard to contentguard.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Delete my ContentGuard account.
  3. In the message, write "Please delete my ContentGuard account and data" and your Gmail address.
  4. We will confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

We only act on requests sent from the Gmail address of the account, so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Gmail address and account ID)
  • Your registered devices and notification tokens
  • Your daily app-usage times and basic device information (brand, model, Android version)
  • Your block lists, schedule and protection settings saved to your account, and Accountability Lock settings
  • Support messages and accountability-partner requests linked to your account (files attached to support requests are removed within 30 days)

What may be kept

  • Records of payments and Premium purchases (including your Gmail address, the amount, the transaction ID and any payment screenshot you uploaded) are kept for up to 5 years for accounting, legal and fraud-prevention purposes, and are not used for anything else.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days after deletion.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account ends any Premium subscription linked to it. No refund is given for the remaining period unless the law requires it.
  • Screen text and web addresses read by ContentGuard on your phone are checked on the phone only and are never stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete for them.
  • You can also just uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not delete your account on our servers; send the request above for that.
